Erlang C calculator for call-centre staffing

Enter your call volume, average handle time and service-level target. The calculator tells you how many agents you need on the phones, how many to schedule after shrinkage, and what service level and wait time to expect.

How to read the result

  • Agents on the phones is the minimum number of people handling calls at the same time to hit your target.
  • Agents to schedule adds shrinkage: breaks, meetings, training and absence. This is the number to put on the roster.
  • Occupancy is how busy agents are. Above about 85-90% for long periods usually leads to burnout and attrition.
  • Average speed of answer (ASA) is the average wait across all calls, including those answered immediately.

The table under the result shows how service level changes with one or two agents more or fewer, which is useful when you have to justify headcount. For a full explanation with a worked example, read Erlang C explained.
