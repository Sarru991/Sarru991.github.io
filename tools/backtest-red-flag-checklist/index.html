Why these checks matter

Most strategies that look great in a backtest fail live for a handful of reasons: costs set too low, signals that accidentally use information from the future, too few trades, and too many parameters tuned to the past. A good backtest is a tool for rejecting ideas cheaply, not for proving them. For the full explanation, read why your EA works in backtest but fails live.
