What I check

Look-ahead bias

Rules that use candles or data that had not closed yet at the time of the trade.

Realistic costs

Spread, commission, slippage and the wide spreads around the rollover hour.

Overfitting

Tests on years the strategy has not seen, and whether the parameters stay stable.

A real edge

Comparison with random entries and a one-bar delay test.
